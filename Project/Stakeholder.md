**Exercise 4: Identify Project Stakeholders**

| Stakeholder                                  | Role                                                                                                | Interest/Expectations                                                                                                        | Influence on the System                                                                                    | How the System Affects the Stakeholder                                                        |
| -------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Maintenance Technicians                      | To perform maintenance and record the completed work                                                | They would expect correct equipment information, documentation and an intuitive way to record and verify maintenance details | Their work determines what maintenance has and needs to be recorded                                        | The system helps them to keep maintenance records organized and keep track of scheduled tasks |
| Quality Assurance / Quality Compliance Staff | Update, access and verify equipment information to ensure required work is completed and documented | They would expect equipment records to be complete, correct and easy to access.                                              | Their requirements determine what information needs to be stored so maintenance activities can be verified | The system helps them check equipment history and verify completed maintenance work           |
| Hospital Management                          | Oversee the complete hospital operations and the proper management of the hospital equipment        | They would expect equipment to be properly maintained and maintenance to be completed on time.                               | They can influence the overall requirements and priorities of the system.                                  | The system gives them more information about equipment status and maintenance activities.     |
