| Risk ID | Risk Description | Possible Cause | Probability | Impact | Mitigation/Response Strategy |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| R1 | Incorrect or missing equipment records | Human data-entry errors, outdated information, or incomplete equipment details | Medium | High | Use input validation, required fields, unique equipment IDs, and regular data audits. Allow authorized staff to update and verify records. |
| R2 | Missed maintenance or calibration deadlines | Incorrect scheduling, system notification failure, or staff overlooking reminders | Medium | High | Implement automated reminders and alerts before due dates. Use dashboard indicators for overdue equipment and escalate critical overdue tasks to supervisors. |
| R3 | Unauthorized access to the system | Weak passwords, poor access control, or compromised user accounts | Medium | High | Implement role-based access control, strong authentication, session timeouts, and audit logs. Restrict sensitive actions to authorized personnel. |
| R4 | System downtime or technical failure | Server problems, software bugs, network outages, or hardware failure | Low/Medium | High | Perform regular backups, system testing, and maintenance. Use backup servers or recovery procedures and provide a manual fallback process for critical maintenance records. |
| R5 | Incorrect calibration or maintenance information being recorded | Technician mistakes, incorrect measurements, or failure to verify completed work | Low/Medium | High | Require technicians to verify submitted records, use standardized forms and measurement units, and maintain an audit trail. Require supervisor approval for critical equipment records. |
