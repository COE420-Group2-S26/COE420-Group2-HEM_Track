**Zeerak**

UC-01
Generate Audit Report
Primary Actor: IT
An IT auditor reviews system logs, with all user actions (create, update, delete) along with corresponding user IDs and timestamps.

UC-02
Schedule Preventive Maintenance
Primary Actor: Maintenance Manager
The manager configures a recurring, monthly preventive maintenance schedule for equipment based on their usage hours.

UC-03
Verify Asset Availability
Primary Actor: Clinical Staff
A nurse could check the system dashboard to verify the real-time status of a piece of equipment (for example, a portable X-ray machine) to ensure it is "Operational" rather than "Out of Service" before fetching it for a patient.

UC-04
Dispatch Upcoming Maintenance Alert
Primary Actor: System
The system automatically dispatches an SMS and email notification to the maintenance team before an equipment’s scheduled calibration date.

UC-05
Register New Asset
Primary Actor: Inventory Manager
The manager inputs a new piece of equipment’s asset ID, serial number, manufacturer, model, purchase date, and department into the system to officially register it in the hospital's inventory.



**Aaditya**

UC-06

Create User Account

Primary Actor: Administrator

The administrator creates a new account for a recently hired technician and assigns them specific role permissions that dictate what modules they can access.

UC-07

Relocate Equipment

Primary Actor: Maintenance Technician

A technician updates the system to record that a patient monitor has been physically transferred from the Emergency Room to the Intensive Care Unit, maintaining its location history.

UC-08

Upload Service Documents

Primary Actor: Maintenance Technician

A technician uploads the manufacturer's service guide and warranty PDF into the system's manual repository for a specific ultrasound machine.

UC-09

Build Maintenance Checklist

Primary Actor: Maintenance Supervisor

The supervisor creates a standardized, 10-point inspection procedure template specifically for hospital beds, which technicians will follow during routine maintenance.

UC-10

Log Repair Metrics

Primary Actor: Maintenance Technician

After fixing a broken centrifuge, the technician inputs that the machine was down for 48 hours and logs the $250 cost of the replacement parts.
