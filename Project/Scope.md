# Define the Project Scope

**Project Objective**

Currently, hospitals keep track of maintenance and calibration for their medical equipment using paper records or Excel sheets. This works okay for a while, but it's very easy to lose track of things. For example, a calibration date gets missed, or nobody remembers when a machine was last serviced. HEM-Track is meant to fix this by giving hospitals one central system where they can store equipment information, keep a history of maintenance/calibration work, and get warned automatically when something is due or overdue for service.

**Target Users**

- Hospital maintenance staff – the people who actually do the maintenance work and log it into the system.
- Equipment/department managers – oversee the equipment inventory and check that everything is being maintained on schedule.

**In-Scope Features**

- We will keep a list of all equipment with basic info like name, location, and department.
- Users can add, edit, or delete equipment records whenever needed.
- The system will record maintenance dates for each piece of equipment.
- It will automatically flag items that are overdue or coming up soon for service.
- There will be a history log showing past maintenance actions for each item.
- Users can search for a specific piece of equipment.
- The system will send simple reminders or notifications when maintenance is coming up or has been missed.

**Out-of-Scope Features**

- This will only be a web app as we are not making a mobile app for now.
- It will only work for a single hospital, not multiple hospitals at once.
- We are not including anything related to budgeting or cost of equipment.
- For user roles, we are just keeping it simple with staff and manager, no complex permission levels.
- For notifications, we are only doing email and in-app alerts, not SMS or push notifications.

**Major Deliverables**

- A working web app with all the in-scope features implemented.
- Documentation of the database schema/data model.
- Basic user documentation that will serve as a guide.
- Planning documents (scope, process model, risk register, stakeholders, feasibility study).
- Final demo/presentation.
