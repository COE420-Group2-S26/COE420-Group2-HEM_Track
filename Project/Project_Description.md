# Project Description

## Problem
Hospitals have a lot of medical equipment that needs regular maintenance and 
calibration to stay safe to use. Right now this is mostly tracked manually 
(paper or spreadsheets), so it's easy to miss a maintenance date or lose track 
of which equipment was last checked.

## Who will use it
- Hospital maintenance staff
- Equipment/department managers

## Main features
- List of all equipment with basic details (name, location, department)
- Track maintenance and calibration dates
- Reminders when equipment is due for maintenance
- Simple log of past maintenance done on each item

## What the system will do
- Add, edit, or remove equipment from the list
- Record maintenance/calibration dates
- Show which equipment is overdue or coming due soon
- Let users search for a specific piece of equipment
